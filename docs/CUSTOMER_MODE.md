# CUSTOMER mode

The role can be assigned only by the local operator and then appears in the permitted primary-mode selector. This provides the role shell, not a completed workshop system. Staff registration, invitations/QR links, scoped owner-approved data grants, live professional sharing, work orders, licensed references, report PDFs and MARKET discounts are PLANNED.

No CUSTOMER role currently grants access to another account's vehicle data or ECU writes. Future access must require vehicle-specific scopes, expiry, explicit owner acceptance, revocation checks and auditable use. No workshop database or real business verification provider is configured.
