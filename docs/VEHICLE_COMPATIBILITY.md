# Vehicle compatibility

Profiles validate make/model/year bounds and optional VIN syntax; they do not decode manufacturer identity, certify engine variants or prove adapter/protocol/part fitment. Every saved vehicle has identityVerified=false. Source provenance and verified catalog/licensed OEM references are required before claiming compatibility. A product title or visually similar vehicle is insufficient.

No production catalog, parts fitment service or real adapter matrix is included in V3. These datasets require separately licensed/verified acquisition; no old catalog data is reused. Unknown compatibility remains unknown. Hardware acceptance records must identify adapter firmware, platform, protocol, ECU, vehicle and permitted operations.
